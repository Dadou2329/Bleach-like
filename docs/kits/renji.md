# Renji Abarai - kit spec            (source: docs/roster/C1-lieutenants-1.md § Renji Abarai; tier S)

## 1. Identity
A brawler's grin over a weapon that finally fits him. Before the Royal Palace: a whip that cuts everything at range
and a bone snake too big to master. After it: two kings — the snake-skull blade on his right arm, the baboon king's
giant arm on his left — and every blow he throws, Hihiō throws too.

## 6. Timelines (60 Hz)
- Hihiō punch: 0-14 wind-up (the giant arm draws back, the disk flares) · 14-24 the punch (jet flame, the fist 3.5 m
  out: 80 dmg, knock 30, launch 8, smash carve r 1.6 along the fist's path) · recovery to 50.
- Orochiō throw: 10 the throw (45 m/s, 40 m on the tail); passing through solid it bores and blows up (explode r 2.4
  every wall entry); on a fighter: 95 dmg + blast r 3, it stays in them (pinned 1.2 s); RMB again: reel — he is
  hauled to it at 30 m/s, a free hit on arrival.
- Whip-snake: 0-12 the tail lashes out (≤ 20 m) · 12 the bite (40 dmg, held) · 12-40 lifted and slammed down (60 dmg,
  crater r 2).
- Zazekka: 0-8 the blade breaks into segments · 8-30 the lash (≤ 22 m, 46 dmg along it); 30 the tip explodes (r 2.4,
  50 dmg); shots along it are rejected.
- Hihi Tenshō: 0-16 the pinch sign · 16 fur clumps land in a ring (r 2.4) round the target · 30 four giant hands rise
  and close (hold 3.5 s, 25 dmg/s crushing); the target can be struck while held.
- Zaga Teppō: 0-10 the thrust (needs a target ≤ 3.2 m ahead, else Hikotsu Taihō) · 10 impaled · 30 the twist ·
  40-90 the burning serpent skull bites: 160 dmg, burn; at ≤ 25 % HP the foe is left an ash husk (dies).
- Hikotsu Taihō (Hihiō): 0-30 charge in the palm · 30-60 an orange-gold beam, 110 dmg.
- Zagai Zekkō: G — the skull goes to the tail end, Hihiō whirls it overhead; the disc grows with the charge (0 → 20 s;
  he can walk, super-armour; a heavy knockdown breaks it) · G again (≥ 4 s): the throw — the disc flies 40 m/s and
  detonates: radius 6 + 0.6 × seconds charged (≤ 18 m), damage 120 + 18 × seconds (≤ 480).

## 8. AI
Calm, Shikai-only until the foe lands something big (≤ 70 % HP), then the Sōō reveal. In Bankai: brawls with the
arm-blade string and Hihiō punches; the umbrella takes projectile spam; Hihi Tenshō when the foe keeps its distance or
dodges, then starts Zagai Zekkō while the hands hold it (throws after 6-10 s, sooner if the foe breaks free and
closes); Zaga Teppō when the foe is close and below half; Orochiō throw at range. Tells: the pinch sign before Hihi
Tenshō, the tail spinning overhead before Zagai Zekkō.
