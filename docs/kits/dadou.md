# Dadou - kit spec            (original character: the game's author; tier S; special roster)

Dadou is not from the manga: he is the person who made REISHI, put into his own game at his request. His look starts
from his photos: the face is kept (thick, wavy dark hair lifted off the forehead and swept to the side, kept short and
short at the sides, the back of the head covered all round; thick straight dark brows; dark brown eyes; a light olive
complexion; a big open grin; no glasses - his wish) and so are the small things (the gold chain with its square gold pendant). The rest
is his brief: an impressive, emperor-like haori, demonic flames in red, violet and black, at least three forms that
each look completely different and each hit harder than the last, and **no balance at all**: like Natacha and Even he
is far stronger than anyone in the canon roster. Nothing here was taken from a fan-work wiki.

## 1. Identity

**"Everything kneels."** Dadou is the Emperor of Hell (地獄の皇帝, Jigoku no Kōtei): a Shinigami who walked down into
Hell of his own will and is the only soul ever to walk back out of its Gates on his own feet. Hell's fire followed him
home - red, violet and black - and he wears it like a court robe. He commands rather than fights: enemies are forced
to their knees, iron pillars fall where he points, chains rise from the ground, the Gates of Hell open behind whoever
he looks at. Every form is a bigger crown: the Emperor, the Demon, the Demon Emperor, the Demon King of the Sixth
Heaven.

His Zanpakutō is **Hajun** (波旬): Mara Pāpīyas, the demon king who rules the sixth heaven of desire. Its spirit is a
towering figure in an imperial robe of black fire, its face hidden behind a demon mask, which bows to no one but him.

behind him, open, and he walks back out of them at full health, one form higher.

cannot be healed while it burns; black fire burns twice as hot and slows.

too); enemies within 6 m of him catch fire.

**Passive (final) - Maō no Iatsu (魔王の威圧, the Demon King's Pressure):** enemies within 16 m are slowed and burn.

## 8. AI profile

Personality: aggressive and unhurried - an emperor who walks in, forces the target to kneel and cuts it. Opener: Ōi.
Signature strings: Ōi → Gochūtekkan → string; Kensaku → Kokuen; the Gates once the target is hurt; Hachinetsu Jigoku
early in the Bankai, Tenka Fubu whenever it is ready. Releases at 90 %, 70 % and 50 % HP.
