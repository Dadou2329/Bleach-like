# Gremmy Thoumeaux - kit spec            (source: docs/roster/H2-sternritter-1.md § 6 Gremmy Thoumeaux; tier A)

## 1. Identity
A child in an oversized coat who never lifts a finger. Whatever he imagines is real: the floor becomes lava, the air
you jumped into was already underwater, your bones are candy, a fortress rises out of the street, a meteor falls.
Calm, curious, a little bored — until you cut his steel; then he wants to crush you, and imagines himself stronger
than his body can hold.

## 2. Loadouts
One card (the Blood War). Not built: Guenael Lee and Shaz Domino (his creations — left out of the game at the user's
request), the novel's brain theft (Tokinada), the gore of the anime-censored bulk-up beyond the torn sleeves.

- String: spike 12 (14, a launch) · bar 14 (16, knock) · block 16 (20, a crater) · ram 18 (24, knock 18, carves).
- Giant Stone Hand: 0-30 the hand forms above · 36 the slam (60, a crater r 3).
- Cookie Bones: 0-24 · disarmed 5 s, slowed 5 s; 18 damage.

- Missile Salvo: 0-20 · eight missiles (22 each, an explosion r 2).
- Lava Surge: 0-30 · a molten front rolling 18 m (14 a second standing in it, the floor melted).
- Stone Pillar Rams: 0-20 · six columns in a fan (20 each, knock 16).

- Stone Fort: 0-50 · a platform 22 m across lifted 6 m on pillars, a parapet round it; everything on it lifted with it. When he dies the pillars are smashed and the platform falls in pieces (even where it fused with a building).
- Meteor: 0-40 · it falls for 3 s, its shadow growing · 180 at the centre (falls off to r 12), a crater r 9.
- Outer Space: 0-30 · 6 s, r 4: 20 a second and slowed inside; leaving it ends it.
- Clone Bombs: each clone dashes in · 30 each, an explosion r 2.5 (the clone is spent).
- Imagined Strength: 0-40 the body swells · ×2.6.

## 7. VFX / callouts
Every creation appears with a 0.2 s prismatic ripple (white core, thin rainbow fringe), then is fully real: stone
`#8A8178`, steel `#9DA6B0`, lava `#FF5A12`, water `#3C8DD8`. Callouts (game-only plain Japanese): "Guremī, 'Za
Vijonarī'.", "…Rakugan.", "Bunshin." (clone), "Inseki." (meteor), "Uchū." (space), "Yōgan." (lava), "Not a single
finger."

## 8. AI
The polite host: the Stone Fort first, lava, the water cube and the crevice; after his steel is cut: rifles, missiles,
the stone hand; at half health: clones, the meteor, outer space, the clone bombs; desperate: Imagined Strength (it
lets go at 5 s). Keeps a middle distance, never brawls.
