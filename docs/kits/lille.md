# Lille Barro - kit spec            (source: docs/roster/H1-vandenreich-leaders.md § Lille Barro; tier A)

## 1. Identity
God's sniper. He stands far off and puts a perfectly round hole through you and every wall between you, and no guard
in the world stops it. Get close and the blade goes through him like water. Hurt him anyway and he only becomes more
of an angel, then a god — until you break the halo that holds him up, or turn his own power back on him.

## 2. Loadouts
One card: the Wahrwelt battle dress (cloak, fur bicorne, the fur pauldron, Diagramm). Not built: the Schutzstaffel
hooded coat (a skin), the partial Jilliel after the Auswählen (one pair of wings, a basic halo: a skin), the story
beats (translating Pernida's mumbles, the five palace cities, the sniper alley of the Wahrwelt).

## 8. AI
A zealot who keeps his distance: the X-Axis and bullets from far off, the stock only when someone gets in. Picks the
most isolated or most hurt enemy. Opens the eye when hurt or cornered (it lights before it opens); on the third, Jilliel:
the wing shots, the barrage, Divine Pressure when crowded, the Six-Hole Blast down long lines, Judgement of God when
they stand still. In the True Form he talks down, uses the Light Ball against decoys and the hidden, spams Sabaki no
Kōmyō, and saves the Trompete for the finale (the fist goes to the beak first).

## 10. Engine needs
Existing: Vollständig flight (`vollFlight`), `DamageOpts.unblockable`, the carve shapes (capsule tunnels, `erase`),
`PlanarSlash`-style cuts, fighters spawned with a `DuelistAI` (the remnants), `lure`/`veiled` (the Light Ball reveals).
New: `Fighter.intangible` + `onPhase` (blows pass through; `game.damage`/`drain` honour it), `DamageOpts.reflected`
(the wound is the victim's own power turned back: set by Freund Schild, Sōgyo no Kotowari, Shiten Kōshun),
`GripDef.two` (a stance can hold a two-handed weapon: the rifle), `vfx/xaxis.ts` (the X-Axis hitscan, bullets, the
scoped shot, wing shots, the six-hole beam, the barrage, the column, Sabaki no Kōmyō, Trompete, the light ball, the
spiral teleport, golden rain).
