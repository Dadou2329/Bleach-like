# Aaroniero Arruruerie - kit spec            (source: docs/roster/E2-espada-2.md § 5, A3 § 7 for Nejibana; tier B; wave W2)

## 1. Identity
A Gillian wearing a dead man's face. In shadow he is Kaien Shiba — the voice, the grin, the spear dance and the
waves of Nejibana; in sunlight the borrowed flesh sloughs off and a glass jar of red liquid with two bickering little
heads is left on his shoulders. Every Hollow he eats makes him more; released, he is all of them at once.

## 2. Loadouts
One loadout (Las Noches): the tall white eight-hole mask over the head, a long white garb, a frilly noble's coat with a
ruffled collar and cuffs, the left hand hidden in one white glove (Glotonería under it). Hole in the left thigh
(hidden). Kaien's aspect keeps Aaroniero's clothes.

## 6. Timelines (60 Hz)
- *True string*: maw bite 0-9 wind, 9-14 active (26, a 1.6 m reach), lash 6-12 (22), chop 6-11 (18), the maw slam
  10-18 (36, smash 120).
- *Devour* (RMB): a 3 m grab 0-12; a downed Hollow or an enemy under 30 % is pulled in and chewed for 0.8 s: 90 damage,
  he heals 15 % and gains a stack (a Hollow: kills it).
- *Spirit Body Fusion* (Q): five red tentacles out to 12 m over 0.4 s; a hit hooks in for 3 s: 12/s drain to him, the
  victim's movement inputs scrambled (its wish turned at random), cut by a heavy blow to him.
- *Zanpakutō Destruction* (R): 6 s armed (the back tentacles glow); a blade blow on him shatters the weapon: disarmed
  10 s (bosses 3 s), the blow does nothing. Once per fight.
- *Kaien string* (sealed): four cuts 22/20/22/30. *Shakkahō*: 30, explode 120.
- *Nejibana dance*: four swings 30/28/30/44 each shedding a water arc (8 m, 18 damage); the 4th raises a wave (a 6 m
  ring, launch). *Crushing tide*: 0-14 the thrust (40, pins 0.6 s), 14-30 the wave behind the target (50, slams it
  forward into the spear). *Rising wave*: a wall 8 m wide, 3 m tall, 0.8 s, lifts (25). *Spiral thrust*: a corkscrew jet
  20 m (70, bore 260).
- *Release* (Glotonería): 0-40 "Kuitsukuse" — the left glove torn off, the maw swells, the mass erupts; full heal.
- *Glotonería*: slams 70/70/90/110 (4 m spheres, smash 300); impale 120 (a 6 m thrust, in shadow); Cero 90 (light);
  horde: up to 4 Hollows; tidal sweep 60 in a 70° fan; swallow 150 over 1.2 s; army: every stack → that many tentacle
  strikes in a 14 m ring (40 each) plus 4 Hollows.

## 8. AI profile
Prefers shadow: when lit he walks for the nearest shade (under a roof, beside a building) and puts Kaien's face on
there; as Kaien he fences, releases Nejibana and drowns the target in waves; arms Zanpakutō Destruction against a sword
user once; devours whatever falls. Releases at 40 % (exposed and cornered), then tentacles and the horde; curls the
tentacles over the capsule when struck there.

## 9. Story hooks

blows out the wall and the sunlight melts the flesh off the capsule; back in shadow the head rebuilds; Glotonería;
the impaling thrust; Shirafune through the capsule; the last sight broadcast to the Espada.
