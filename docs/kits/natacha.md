# Natacha - kit spec            (original character, a tribute; tier S; special roster)

Natacha is not from the manga. She was added at the request of the game's author as a tribute to someone dear to
them, so her look starts from that person's photos (the face is kept: the copper hair, the freckles, the dark brown
eyes and the wide smile) and her powers are original, built to sit inside the Bleach world's rules (Shikai, Bankai,
Kidō, Shunpo) while being **deliberately unbalanced**: she is the strongest fighter in the game, and every form is
stronger than the last. Nothing here was taken from a fan-work wiki.

## 1. Identity

**"The night sky answers her."** Natacha is the Hoshiyomi (星読み, the Star Reader) of the Royal Guard: warm,
graceful, smiling even in a fight, and overwhelming. Her freckles are said to be stars that fell on her face; her
Zanpakutō is the morning star. Each release adds a layer of the sky - marks of light, then the whole night with its
meteors and constellations, and finally the dawn itself.

## 2. Loadouts

One loadout (her peak). No eras.

F climbs the ladder (Sealed → Shikai → Bankai → Final); F in the final form seals everything back (the sky returns).

**Passive (all forms) - Myōjō no Kago (明星の加護, the Morning Star's Protection):** the first time she would die,
the morning star rises behind her: she comes back at 60 % HP with a burst of light that blows everyone away.

**Passive - Mitsuboshi (三つ星, Three Stars):** the three stones of her necklace are her three seals: one lights
with each release. (Visual: the stones glow on the model.)

## 8. AI profile

Aggression high, patience medium, keeps mid range (8-25 m) in the released forms and rushes in sealed; guard via
Hoshiyomi when rushed. Releases at 0.95 / 0.75 / 0.5 of HP (she climbs fast). Favourite strings: Shikai marks →
Seiza-giri; Bankai Kuro-boshi → Ryūseigun; Final Hoshi no Toki → Seigun Ranbu → Chōshinsei. Akatsuki when the target
is in front and under 70 % HP. Quirk: a smile and a bow before the fight (opener: Hoshiyomi).

## 9. Story hooks

None (original character). Her card sits in the roster with a gold "special" frame, and the start screen's
**Special characters wiki** page explains her Zanpakutō, releases and powers.
