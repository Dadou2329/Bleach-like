# Even - kit spec            (original character, a tribute; tier S; special roster)

Even is not from the manga. He was added at the request of the game's author as a tribute to someone dear to them,
so his look starts from that person's photos: the face is kept (dark hair swept up into a soft quiff with a side
parting, round clear glasses, dark eyes, a fair complexion and a big open grin), and so are the little things (the
thin violet cord on his left wrist, the round black lens he wears as a badge). His powers are original, built to sit
inside the Bleach world's rules (Shikai, Bankai, Kidō, Shunpo) while being **deliberately unbalanced**: like Natacha
he is far stronger than anyone in the canon roster, and every form is stronger than the last. Nothing here was taken
from a fan-work wiki.

## 1. Identity

**"Nothing gets past his eyes."** Even is the Daikidōchō (大鬼道長), the Grand Kidō Chief of the Kidō Corps: a sunny
genius in round glasses who casts Hadō in the nineties without a chant while he is still talking, then pushes his
glasses up and grins. His Zanpakutō is a mirror: whatever it sees clearly, it can reflect, bend, focus - and judge.
Each release goes one step further from "the man who sees" to "the light itself": lenses and prisms, then a world of
still mirror-water where every reflection obeys him, and finally light that reaches everything at once.

F climbs the ladder (Sealed → Shikai → Bankai → Final); F in the final form seals everything back (the lake drains,
the sky returns).

**Passive (all forms) - Shingan (心眼, the Mind's Eye):** illusions do not work on him: he cannot be blinded,
inverted, turned against his friends or have his steps scrambled.

**Passive (all forms) - Kagami-gawari (鏡代わり, the Mirror Stand-in):** the first time he would die, it was a
reflection that was struck: it shatters like glass and he steps out from behind the attacker at 50 % HP.

**Passive - Daikidōchō (大鬼道長, the Grand Kidō Chief):** every Kidō is cast without its incantation at full power.

## 8. AI profile

Aggression high, patience high, keeps mid range (6-25 m) and plays the caster in sealed (Byakurai, Kuyō Shibari
into Kurohitsugi); Dankū or Hansha when rushed. Releases at 0.9 / 0.72 / 0.5 of HP. Favourite strings: sealed Kuyō
Shibari → Kurohitsugi; Shikai Senkō → Kakudai; Bankai Kagami-watari → Senkyō Kōrō, Kyōzō early; final Hyakkakyō →
Issen, Jōhari no Kagami once the target has dealt a lot of damage (or is under 60 % HP). Quirk: he pushes his
glasses up and grins before the fight (opener: the glint, then Senkō in the Shikai).
