# Jugram Haschwalth - kit spec            (source: docs/roster/H1-vandenreich-leaders.md § Jugram Haschwalth; tier A)

## 1. Identity
The scales never lose: every wound he takes, someone else pays for, and the blade finishes it. Calm, economical,
a counter-fighter who stands still and lets them come.

## 2. Loadouts
One card (Grandmaster, the Blood War). No Vollständig in canon; the night swap and the anime's hAschwAlth are the ladder.

## 8. AI
Stands still and lets enemies come (counter range 3-6 m); raises the shield when shots come; tips the scales when
the store passes 150; Fortune Inversion before a Judgment; Night below 55 % HP, hAschwAlth below 30 %.
