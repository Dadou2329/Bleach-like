# Askin Nakk Le Vaar - kit spec            (source: docs/roster/H1-vandenreich-leaders.md § Askin Nakk Le Vaar; tier B)

## 1. Identity
Slow, smug, deadly — and immune to whatever you just hit him with. He never fights fair: he drops poison where you
will stand, runs, lets you chase him into it, and talks the whole time. Stay on him long enough and your blows stop
working; then he walks back in, polite, and tells you that was fatal.

## 2. Loadouts
One card: Wahrwelt Askin (the cloakless outfit he fought Yoruichi, Yūshirō and Urahara in). Not built: the white
Sternritter cloak of the first invasion and the Schutzstaffel hooded coat (skins), the burned state (shirt gone), the
partial (anime) Hasshein.

## 8. AI
Opens by dropping a Gift Bad and a couple of mines, then backs off (keeps 9-14 m) and shoots; when the target closes in
he runs and lobs a Gift Ball back over his shoulder. Tastes right after a blow lands. Once immune to the target he walks
in and brawls. Analyse when one enemy has hurt him a lot. Hasshein below 50 % (or when an immunity breaks): the Deluxe
first, the Bereich, then the Ring on a tough target and the swarm.
