# Szayelaporro Granz - kit spec            (source: docs/roster/E2-espada-2.md § 4, 4a, 4b; tier A; wave W2)

## 1. Identity
The scientist who never fights fair: everything he does is prepared, recorded and turned back on you. He keeps his
distance, studies you, makes your own power useless, sets your copies on you and plays with a doll of your body.

## 2. Loadouts
One loadout (Hueco Mundo, the palace): the Espada uniform (a long white shirt-tunic closed to the neck, three black
stripes from the collar, white gloves, cropped trousers, the sword at thigh height on two frilly light-purple cords),
pink chin-length hair swept to the right, two shaved lines on the left side, rectangular bone-framed glasses (his
mask). Later: the cape after he changes clothes (skin), the Privaron lab coat (skin), the Jailer of Hell (an era
loadout of its own: chains, horns, the charred arm; not built yet).

## 6. Timelines (60 Hz)
- *String* (sealed LMB): thrust 0-7 wind, 7-13 active (16 dmg), the wrist flick 6-12, the rising cut 6-13 (launch 9),
  the spinning slash 8-20 (22 dmg); one-handed, the left hand on the hip.
- *Rokureichū* (RMB sealed): 0-8 the needle drawn back, 8-14 the thrust (10 dmg, reach 2.6 m): a hit plants the bug.
  Data: each technique a tagged enemy starts (an action that costs ≥ 6 reiatsu) is one sighting; three sightings
  complete it (a "DATA COMPLETE" tag over them).
- *Immunization* (passive): a hit from a tagged enemy while it uses a completed technique (its current action, or an
  energy hit within 3 s of starting it) is nullified: pink hexagons flash where it struck.
- *Fracción* (Q sealed): 0-20 a snap of the fingers; Lumina and Verona drop out of a hatch of light beside him (1 m
  tall, 160 HP each, a feeble slap); one call each 18 s while they live.
- *Trap panel* (E sealed): 0-10 the remote; a ring glows round the target's feet for 0.35 s, then a pit (1.4 m wide,
  3.5 m deep) opens under them: they drop, 25 damage at the bottom.
- *Power-control device* (R sealed): 0-14 the remote held up; a pink scan-grid dome 12 m round where he stands for
  14 s: tagged enemies inside cannot release (sealT), hit 25 % weaker, and an analysed technique fizzles as it starts.
- *Release* (F): 0-20 the blade into the mouth, a pink glow from the throat; 20-40 the body swells, fluid and mist burst;
  40-60 the anemone of tentacles; 60 the form (full heal).
- *Lashes* (LMB Fornicarás): branches lash out from the wings at 0.12 s: 18/18/20/26 dmg, reach 3.5/5/6.5/8 m.
- *Burrowing branch* (RMB): 0-12 a branch into the ground; it travels under the earth (a ridge of upheaval) at 30 m/s;
  out under the target: 30 dmg, held 1.2 s (bosses 0.6 s), lifted.
- *Carbon-Copy* (Q): 0-24 the fountain; 24-90 black rain over 12 m; each enemy wet gets a copy (40 % of its life, his
  side, the duel AI, black and blank-eyed) — two per enemy, four in all. The snap: every copy bursts.
- *Teatro de Titere* (E): a wing reaches the nearest enemy within 4.5 m: swallowed (hidden, held 0.8 s), spat out
  (knocked down, 20 dmg); a strand swells purple and pops: the doll (25 s). Flick 18 dmg; *estómago* 90 dmg and
  staggered; Achilles: down 1.4 s, slowed 6 s; arm tendon: disarmed 5 s; Gran Final 200 dmg, doll gone. Any
  heavy blow to him drops it.
- *Telon Balon* (R): 0-10 the wings fold round him; ×0.12 damage, no knockback, a slow float up; 4 s or R again.
- *Gabriel* (F Fornicarás): 0-14 a branch to the navel (6 m): the egg (one at a time, one rebirth a match). On his
  death: 2 s later the host's belly swells, it loses 35 % of its life and is stunned 1.5 s, and he climbs out beside
  it at 50 % life.
- *Eat a Fracción* (G): the nearest Fracción within 10 m waddles to him; 0.9 s: swallowed whole, +35 % life.

## 8. AI profile
Keeps his distance (ranged); opens with the Fracción and a Rokureichū thrust; lays the trap panel under a rushing
target; puts up the device once a technique is analysed; releases at 55 % life; then Carbon-Copy, Telon Balon against
big attacks (when the enemy starts a technique with a long wind-up, or his life is low), Teatro when they come close,
and once he holds a doll he plays with it (flick, stomach, tendons, then the Gran Final). Lays Gabriel early. Eats a
Fracción below 45 % life. A heavy blow to the head makes him furious for a while (vanity).

## 9. Story hooks
Renji and Uryū in the palace (the slide, the device, the Fracción ambush, Seele Schneider through the throat doing
nothing); the release and the copy brawl; Teatro on Uryū and Renji; Mayuri's dummy organs, Gabriel out of Nemu, the
Superhuman Drug; the Hell one-shot (later loadout).
