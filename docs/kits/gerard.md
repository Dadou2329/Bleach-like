# Gerard Valkyrie - kit spec            (source: docs/roster/H1-vandenreich-leaders.md § Gerard Valkyrie; tier A)

## 1. Identity
The Miracle with a sword. A laughing knight who wants to be hit: every wound he takes is the people's fear given form,
and fear makes him bigger. Beat him down and he stands back up taller than the towers, healed, and swats the street
flat. Cut him in half and his heart pulls the halves back together in gold. Only freezing his hope, toppling the giant
by the foot, or burning out the last light ends him.

## 2. Loadouts
One card: the Wahrwelt battle gear (winged helmet, white cloak with the Hagal rune, the red cape, X-belt, round shield,
Hoffnung). Not built: the Schutzstaffel hooded coat (a skin), the partial Vollständig after the second Auswählen (red
feathered wings and a Zeichen halo: a skin), the story beats (rising out of Yhwach's shadow, the Royal Guard fights,
catching Pernida).

Passives:
- **The Miracle** (奇跡): a meter 0-100 fed by every wound (a wound of 1 % of his health = 1.25), by being outnumbered
  (+3 a second per enemy beyond the first within 30 m) and by being near death (+6 a second below 30 % health). Full:
  the next Godly Size step. Above half: control statuses (slow, roots, held arms, scrambled steps, seals) wear off
  three times faster, and the hidden are found anyway ("because it was impossible"): his targeting ignores veils and
  lures.
- **Damage → size**: a lethal blow while a Godly Size step remains becomes that step instead (the fatal wound, too, is
  turned into size).
- **Hope Infusion** (希望の剣): a melee blow that meets Hoffnung — from the front while he is swinging it — is turned
  back: he takes nothing, the attacker takes ×2.5 their own blow (`reflected`). **Freezing** stops it: an ice blow
  freezes the blade instead (6 s, no reflection), a second one cuts it in half (no sword for 10 s).
- **Regeneration**: a single wound of 12 % of his health or more is a limb lost and regrown in a flash of light — red
  marking lines on the arms, and +5 % strength (four times at most).
- **Heart of the Soul King**: the revivals; never appears in The Almighty's visions (Yhwach's and Haschwalth's
  foresight skip him).
- **A giant is a big, slow target**: a heavy blow at his feet while he strides **topples** him (down 2.5 s, every 15 s
  at most); the whole body is one big hurtbox.

## 8. AI
Wants to be hit: walks in, trades, laughs; cape-walls when chased at range; grows when the meter fills; at Godly
Size: area denial (backhand, stomp, Branch Breaker), throws buildings at targets beyond 20 m, gale vs fliers, grabs
the small ones. Never retreats. Aschtonig: beams and the slash wake.
