# Candice Catnipp - kit spec            (source: docs/roster/H3-sternritter-2.md § Candice Catnipp; tier A)

## 1. Identity
Lightning wherever she points. A loud, vain, short-fused brawler who slaps and kicks with crackling hands and heeled
boots, turns into a bolt to get out of the way, calls the sky down on whoever she dislikes and, when someone dirties
her hair, goes bigger and dumber; in Barbarriel she is a six-winged storm with a sword in each hand that burns out fast.

## 2. Loadouts
One card (the Blood War). Not built: the Zombie (Giselle's) and the regrown arm as playable states, the Medallion.

## 8. AI
Opens at range with Thunderbolt and Sky Strike; brawls when the target closes, Lightning Engulf when it gets behind her;
Lightning Jump out of big swings; Rage Discharge when held; Galvano Blast often (spammed in a Rage); Barbarriel below
60 % (or early in a Rage) — swords up close, javelins at range, Electrocution as the finisher; burns out and backs off
while Exhausted.
