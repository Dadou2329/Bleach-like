# Meninas McAllon - kit spec            (source: docs/roster/H3-sternritter-2.md § Meninas McAllon; tier B+)

## 1. Identity
Soft-spoken, dreamy, polite — and the strongest arms of the Bambies. She swells a fist to three times its size and
puts people through buildings; when she cannot reach you, she picks up the building. Nothing she does is fast or
clever: it is all simply too heavy to stop.

## 2. Loadouts
One card (the Blood War). Not built: the mind-controlled state (PePe's Love Kiss), the zombie (that is Giselle's: zombie
Meninas joins the dead Giselle calls up), the novel's captivity.

## 8. AI
Closes relentlessly; punches up close (the hammer fist when the target is cornered), Diamond Push at mid range,
Head-Grab when it can reach, throws chunks at a target that keeps its distance, Muscle Up below 70 %, Pornipora below
55 %, Building Drop in Pornipora; strikes a turned back.
