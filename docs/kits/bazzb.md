# Bazz-B - kit spec            (source: docs/roster/H2-sternritter-1.md § 1 Bazz-B; tier A)

## 1. Identity
"One finger is enough." A loud street-brawler with a burning hand who escalates one finger at a time — every count
is a bigger fire, until the whole hand burns. The finger count *is* the move.

## 2. Loadouts
One card (the Blood War, coat-only after the Hitsugaya fight). Skins not built: the child Bazzard, the hooded cloak,
the Lichtreich uniform.

## 8. AI
Never retreats; opens with BF1 from range ("one finger is enough"); adds a finger as his HP drops (BF2 below 85 %,
BF3 below 70 %, BF4 below 55 %, Full Fingers below 40 %); Vollständig below 50 %; collar-grabs when close; Burning
Stomp clears fields.
