# Roster A2 — Ichigo's allies

Design dossiers for Ichigo's core companions: **Rukia Kuchiki** (13th Division lieutenant → captain; ice Zanpakutō + the group's deepest Kidō library), **Orihime Inoue** (Shun Shun Rikka — six fairy spirits that "reject" events: shields, restoration, a splitting attack), **Yasutora "Chad" Sado** (Fullbring arms: Brazo Derecha de Gigante / Brazo Izquierda del Diablo, every stage), **Uryū Ishida** (Quincy archer across the whole series, from Kojaku to Sternritter "A" and the anime-only Vollständig) and **Ryūken Ishida** (his father, the "Last Quincy").

Conventions used below
- Heights → voxels at **32 vox/m** (rounded). Proportion notes assume a head height of ≈ 1/7 of body height for adults (Rukia ≈ 1/6.5).
- Hex colors are **estimates sampled from wiki reference images**: median of 7×7 px patches and median-cut palettes taken from the **official TYBW anime character sheets** (Rukia, Orihime, Sado, Uryū, Ryūken adult + teen), anime frames (day-lit ones preferred; night/red-lit frames only used for shape) and official digital-color manga pages. Values marked **(est.)** were judged by eye rather than sampled — verify before final texturing.
- Canon flags: **[manga]** original manga; **[anime]** anime depiction of manga events; **[anime-only]** anime-original material — **[filler]** for the 2004–12 filler arcs, **[TYBW-anime ep N]** for the 2022–26 Thousand-Year Blood War adaptation (eps 367+; part 2 = 380–392, part 3 = 393–406, part 4 *The Calamity* = 407+, which adds long anime-original stretches: Uryū's Vollständig, Rukia's Bankai vs. Soul-King Yhwach, Orihime's Rokuten Shishun); **[movie-only]**, **[novel]**, **[game-only]**; **[extrapolation]** = a game-design proposal that canon never shows. Unflagged statements are manga canon.
- Engine tags use the brief's primitive vocabulary in `[brackets]`. Needs **not** covered by the brief's list are tagged `[NEW: …]` and collected in **§6**.
- Sources (bleach.fandom.com via the MediaWiki API, raw wikitext incl. chapter/episode citations; paraphrased): Rukia_Kuchiki (+ /Anime_Continuity, /Image_Gallery), Sode_no_Shirayuki_(Zanpakutō_spirit), Orihime_Inoue (+ /Anime_Continuity, /Image_Gallery), Yasutora_Sado (+ /Anime_Continuity, /Image_Gallery), Uryū_Ishida (+ /Anime_Continuity, /Image_Gallery), Ryūken_Ishida (+ /Image_Gallery), Kidō + the individual spell pages (Sai, Hainawa, Geki, Rikujōkōrō, Byakurai, Shakkahō, Sōkatsui, Sōren Sōkatsui), Gintō + Template:GintōSpells (Heizen, Gritz, Wolke, Sprenger), Heilig_Pfeil, Licht_Regen, Seele_Schneider, Hirenkyaku, Blut, Ransōtengai, Sklaverei, Quincy, Quincy:_Vollständig, Quincy:_Letzt_Stil, Sanrei_Glove, Sternritter, Schutzstaffel, Äs_Nödt, Fullbringer.

## 1. Rukia Kuchiki (朽木 ルキア) — 13th Division Lieutenant → Captain; the Shinigami who started Ichigo's story

- **Wiki:** https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki (Sode no Shirayuki & Hakka no Togame live in its *Zanpakutō* section) · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki/Anime_Continuity · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Sode_no_Shirayuki_(Zanpakut%C5%8D_spirit) · spells: https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Kid%C5%8D
- **Roster tier:** **S** — co-protagonist; a complete three-stage sword kit (sealed + Kidō → Shikai → Bankai) plus a "white body" stance; the flagship for the game's ice material and freeze-then-shatter destruction. Ship first.
- **Canon power: 8/10** — In TYBW she lowers her own body to absolute zero to overpower Äs Nödt (a Sternritter who had already beaten Byakuya), forces him into Vollständig and then freezes him into nothing with Bankai [manga ch.567–570]; [TYBW-anime ep.411] her Shikai/Bankai briefly freeze Soul-King-empowered Yhwach's torrent of darkness and Yhwach himself. Pre-TYBW she sits around 6 (one dance kills the Arrancar Di Roy; she kills the 9th Espada Aaroniero). Her ceiling is limited by self-damage at absolute zero.
- **Body:** **144 cm → 46 vox**, 33 kg. Very petite and slender: narrow shoulders (~10 vox), thin wrists and ankles, large eyes, ≈6.5 heads tall (head ≈7 vox). Silhouette keys: **black, chin-length hair that flares outward at the tips, with one long lock hanging between the eyes** (keep that lock in every era); oversized black sleeves/hakama that make her look even smaller; a katana long for her frame (sheathed, it reaches from chest to knee). Shikai adds a **long white ribbon** trailing from the pommel; Bankai roughly doubles her width (tall ice collar, angular ice shoulder plates, floor-length wide sleeves, big looping back ribbons).

- **Look by form/outfit:**
  - **Classic Shinigami (2001):** plain black shihakushō, white collar and obi, tabi + zōri; chin-length hair with the lock between the eyes; no badge; sword at the left hip.
  - **Gigai / human disguise:** Karakura High girls' uniform (white blouse, red neck bow, grey skirt) or Yuzu's borrowed dresses. Props: the red **Gokon Tekkō** glove with a flaming-skull emblem (punches a soul out of its body), Chappy Soul Candy dispenser, flip-phone Denreishinki.
  - **Lieutenant (17 months later → early TYBW):** hair cut into a **bob** that frames the face; **left sleeve cut back to the upper arm** with the **lieutenant badge** (plaque with the 13th Division snowdrop emblem on a white armband) tied around it; **white fingerless tekkō** from knuckles to above the elbow on both arms (like Byakuya's); TYBW sheet ties the white obi in a front bow.
  - **Ōken clothing (TYBW, after Royal Guard training):** a long white shawl wound around the head and hanging past the shoulders, fluffy white earmuffs, a dark visor over the eyes, an ankle-length white cloak over the shihakushō. It is made from the bones and hair of the Royal Guard (survives the 72 barriers). **It shatters when she releases Bankai.**

  - **Bankai — Hakka no Togame:** hair and irises turn white; skin goes frost-pale; white ankle-length kimono with fine line patterns and glittering frost; **tall flaring stand-up collar, hems and angular shoulder pieces of transparent ice**; very long, wide sleeves; **long ribbons tied at her back forming several big loops** that trail metres behind; **a small ice flower at the centre of the chest**; **a half-crown of ice around the back of the head** (the anime draws it as a spiky ice-crystal cluster with hanging icicle tassels on the side of the head); the sword is now clear ice. Body sparkles; footsteps and breath shed cold mist.
  - **Captain (10 years after Yhwach, ch.685):** hair grown long (classic cut, well past the shoulders, lock still between the eyes); **captain haori** with long wide sleeves, **oxblood lining**, black lozenge band along the hem, "十三" on the back; no badge.
  - **12 years after (NO BREATHES FROM HELL one-shot):** long hair in a **side ponytail** with a purple tie.
  - **Alt costumes:** **Dark Rukia** [movie-only: *Fade to Black*] — lavender hair and eyes, barefoot, different robe, one-handed giant double-edged scythe. **Sode no Shirayuki spirit** [filler] — see Abilities → other media.

  - **Bankai:** the Shikai shape rendered in **transparent ice** (translucent voxels, refraction), white hilt.
  - **Gadgets:** Gokon Tekkō, Denreishinki (radar + phone), Kikanshinki (memory-replacement device), Soul Candy dispenser (Chappy).
- **Forms & states (ordered):**
  1. **Gigai (depowered)** — no sword; weak Kidō + Hakuda; Kon/Chappy helpers. *(story prologue only)*
  2. **Sealed** — katana, Shunpo, full Kidō list.
  3. **Shikai — "Mae, Sode no Shirayuki"** (she holds the sword out and turns it through a full counter-clockwise circle) — white blade, the three dances, ranged freezing, ice paths.
  4. **White body (true Shikai power)** — not a new release but a state: her body temperature drops below freezing; whatever she touches freezes; she can step down to absolute zero (−273.15 °C), safe for ≤ 4 s; she must warm back up slowly.
  5. **Bankai — "Bankai, Hakka no Togame"** — a pillar of white cold mist erupts; outfit transforms (Ōken garb destroyed); absolute-zero field; touching her freezes people solid; hard time limit and a slow, dangerous thaw. *(manga: nearly immobile; [TYBW-anime ep.411]: moves at almost normal speed by the Yhwach fight.)*
