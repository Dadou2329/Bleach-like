# Roster A3 — Urahara Shop & the Shiba clan

Design dossiers for the exiles of the **Urahara Shop** — **Kisuke Urahara** (ex-12th captain, inventor, Kidō master, Bankai *Kannonbiraki Benihime Aratame*), **Yoruichi Shihōin** (the "Flash Goddess", Shunkō, cat form, TYBW beast form) and **Tessai Tsukabishi** (ex-Grand Kidō Chief, forbidden Kidō) — and the fallen noble **Shiba clan**: **Kūkaku** (fireworks, Kidō, the Flower Crane Cannon), **Ganju** (sand "earth magic", chili fireworks, Bonnie the boar) and the late **Kaien** (Nejibana water trident), plus the West Gate giant **Jidanbō Ikkanzaka**.

Conventions used below
- Heights → voxels at **32 vox/m** (rounded). Giants/colossi get a recommended coarser build resolution.
- Hex colors are **estimates sampled from wiki reference images** (dominant-color clusters from anime screenshots, corrected for scene lighting) and from the **official TYBW-anime design art hosted on the wiki** ("Urahara Anime Fullbody", "Yoruichi Anime Fullbody", "Ganju Anime Fullbody", the storyboard *CALAMITYBenihimeNotes* and the *CALAMITYYoruichiSheet* lightning-coverage sheet). Old-anime (2004–12) and TYBW-anime (2022–26) palettes differ noticeably for Urahara and Yoruichi — both are given. Anything tagged **(verify)** should be checked against model sheets.
- Canon flags: **[manga]** canon (unflagged = manga), **[anime-only]** (2004–12 fillers), **[TYBW-anime]** (additions/depictions only in the 2022–26 adaptation; Part 4 "The Calamity" eps 405–408 cover the Askin fight), **[novel]** (*Can't Fear Your Own World*), **[movie-only]**, **[game-only]**.
- Engine tags use the brief's primitive vocabulary in `[brackets]`. Needs **not** covered by the brief's list are tagged `[NEW: …]` and collected in **§8**.
- Sources (bleach.fandom.com via MediaWiki API, paraphrased): Kisuke_Urahara (+ /Anime_Continuity, /Expanded_Universe, /Battles_&_Events, /Image_Gallery), Yoruichi_Shihōin (+ /Anime_Continuity, /Battles_&_Events, /Image_Gallery), Tessai_Tsukabishi (+ /Anime_Continuity), Kūkaku_Shiba, Ganju_Shiba, Jidanbō_Ikkanzaka (+ *Jidanbō Ikkanzaka vs. Ichigo Kurosaki*), Kaien_Shiba, Metastacia, Bonnie, Koganehiko_and_Shiroganehiko, Shiba_Clan, Shihōin_Clan, every page in *Category:Kidō* (practitioner lists), Benihime technique pages, Shunkō (+ Raijin Senkei, Raijū Senkei, Bakuen Musō, Mukyū Shunkō), Utsusemi, Raiōken, Taketonbo, Tesshō, Seppa, Renkan_Seppa_Sen, Shiba-shiki_Shaka_Sendan:_Senpen_Banka, Kakaku_Taihō, Reishukaku, Kagizaki, Juppon/Banzai_Jidanda_Matsuri, Hōgyoku, Gigai, Senkaimon, Gokon_Tekkō, Shin'eiyaku, Hohō, Hakuda, Seireitei, Sekkiseki.
