## Shared Gotei-13 captain baseline (applies to all four unless a dossier says otherwise)

- `Shunpo (瞬歩, Flash Step)` — Shinigami high-speed step on **Shift**; double-tap with lock-on = appear behind. Captains also stand/walk on reishi in mid-air (**Space** air-step). [Mobility, Teleport]
- `Zanjutsu / Hakuda` — sword and unarmed fundamentals; each dossier lists the personal level. [MeleeArc]
- `Kidō (鬼道)` — Hadō (attack), Bakudō (binding/barriers), Kaidō (healing). Full incantation = strongest; *Eishōhaki* (no incantation) = faster but weaker; *Nijū Eishō* = two interleaved chants. Per Kubo (Klub Outside Q&A #855) most captains can heal physical injuries with Kaidō — **Zaraki is an explicit exception**. Map to **Tab** wheel. [Projectile, Beam, Bind, Shield, Heal/Reject]
- **Captain haori:** white, division number in black kanji on the upper back inside the Gotei rhombus; the **underside colour is captain-specific** (only Ukitake's — crimson — is documented here). A band of rhombus/diamond pattern along the hem is the usual anime look **(verify per character)**.
- **Gentei Reiin / Gentei Kaijo (限定霊印 / 限定解除):** in the Human World, captains and lieutenants are stamped with a limiter seal (their **division flower**) that cuts output by ~80%; with Soul Society's authorization the seal dissolves (×5 power, reiatsu pillar). Game rule proposal: Human-World maps start captain-class characters "limited" until a story beat or meter unlocks **G → Gentei Kaijo**. [Stance, NEW: GenteiLimiter]
- **Bankai theft (TYBW):** a Sternritter **Medallion** fired at a *released* Bankai pulls it apart in five dark streams and stores it; the owner loses contact with the spirit. Urahara's **Shin'eiyaku** pill (absorbed by touch) briefly **Hollowfies** a Bankai, making it poison to the Quincy holding it, so it tears free and returns (Hitsugaya's mask & horns). Applies here only to Hitsugaya (Kenpachi had no Bankai yet, Mayuri never released his during the first invasion, Ukitake never showed one). [Status: Bankai theft]
- **Zombification (Giselle Gewelle):** captain-class zombies turn **reddish-skinned**; if zombified while still alive they keep "fresh cells" (faster) but lose free will. Mayuri's blood-reorganization drug flips control to him; his preservation vats undo it at the cost of lifespan. [Status: mind control, NEW: AllegianceSwap]
- **Konsō Reisai & Hell:** twelve years after a captain dies, the funeral rite sends their reiatsu-dense soul to **Hell**; captains there gain epithets and are physically altered (Ukitake = **"Kamikake"**, Sōgyo no Kotowari grown several times larger).

## Tōshirō Hitsugaya (日番谷 冬獅郎) — Captain, 10th Division

- **Wiki:** https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/T%C5%8Dshir%C5%8D_Hitsugaya (+ /Anime_Continuity) · technique pages: Hyōrinmaru_(Zanpakutō_spirit) (+ /Anime_Continuity), Tensō_Jūrin, Ryūsenka, Sennen_Hyōrō, Hyōten_Hyakkasō, Gunchō_Tsurara, Hyōryū_Senbi, Zanhyō_Ningyō, Shikai_Hyōketsu, Rokui_Hyōketsujin, Ryōjin_Hyōheki, Shinkū_Tasō_Hyōheki, Tenteikūra, Shin'eiyaku, Medallion · battles: vs. Gin Ichimaru; vs. Shawlong Koufang; vs. Luppi Antenor; vs. Tier Harribel; vs. Yukio Hans Vorarlberna; Hitsugaya & Rangiku vs. Bazz-B; vs. Cang Du; Battle of the Zombies; The Gotei 13 & The Visored vs. Gerard Valkyrie · TYBW anime notes ep. 15–16, 22–23, 43–44 (#381–382, #388–389, #409–410).
- **Roster tier:** **S** (core, ship first). Fan favourite with four instantly different silhouettes (child Shikai, winged Bankai, Hollowfied Bankai, adult Complete Bankai) and the flagship for the ice/water/weather simulation; also required as a zombie boss in Mayuri's story.
- **Canon power:** **8/10** (child Bankai ≈7, Complete Bankai ≈8.5). Complete Bankai froze a Godly-Size Gerard solid with Shikai Hyōketsu, froze his thrown shield in mid-air and cut Hoffnung in half after switching off its damage-reflection; the child Bankai killed Shawlong (after limit release) and Cang Du, entombed Harribel and Luppi. Lost to Aizen twice, to Bazz-B while Bankai-less, and to Mayuri's drugs as a zombie.
- **Body:** 133–134 cm → **~43 vox** @32 (**~64** @48); 28 kg. Child proportions (~5 heads): big head, narrow shoulders, short limbs — he is constantly mistaken for an elementary schooler. The 1.4 m nodachi is **longer than he is**; its hilt and star guard stick up behind his **right shoulder**. Silhouette keys: tall spiky white hair, the green bandolier sash with a round star clip, **sleeveless** haori over black kosode sleeves, frost particles. TYBW: lankier, longer fringe. **Adult (Complete Bankai):** no official height — a tall, lanky young man who can pass as his own "older brother"; model at **~175 cm → ~56 vox (84 @48)** (estimate, verify against art), same white spikes but longer, sharper older face.

## Research notes — discrepancies, open questions, things not found

- **"Hōten Hyōjū" — not found.** Full-text wiki searches for "Hōten Hyōjū", "Hyōjū", "Hoten", "Hyoju", "宝天" and "氷獣" return nothing relevant (the only kanji hits are unrelated 獣 characters in Ira / Quimera Parca), and it is absent from the technique lists of every TYBW episode page checked (ep. 9–10, 15–16, 20, 22–23, 29–30, 34–36, 39–48). Most likely a mix-up with **Hyōten Hyakkasō (氷天百華葬)**; treat it as that unless someone has a source.
- **Tenteikūra is not a Hitsugaya technique.** It is Bakudō #77 (mass telepathic broadcast); canon practitioners are Isane, Tōsen and Rangiku. Hitsugaya's link: after Cang Du steals his Bankai he orders Rangiku to broadcast the warning with it.
- **"Mashiro-tweaked Bankai" for Mayuri — no such thing found.** The TYBW alternate Bankai is **Konjiki Ashisogi Jizō: Matai Fukuin Shōtai** (Kaizō Bankai), built from Pernida data; no connection to Mashiro Kuna.
- **"Ice clones"** — canon shows one Zanhyō Ningyō at a time (he says the trick usually works once per opponent).
- **Adult form + Hyōten Hyakkasō** — canon only shows Hyōten Hyakkasō in the child Bankai; the adult's "instant freezing" is flash-freezing + Shikai Hyōketsu. The instant-bloom version in the kit is an extrapolation.
- **Rokui Hyōketsujin:** the character page says five crystals in a pentagon; the technique page and the Bazz-B battle (six sigils) say **six in a hexagon** — the name means "six" → use six.
- **Shikai Hyōketsu:** technique page = the space marked by **four paces**; one plot summary paraphrases it as "four seconds"; Brave Souls says "four steps". Use four landing markers.
- **Konjiki Ashisogi Jizō poison radius:** character page ≈ **200 m**; the Uryū battle page says "100-mile radius" (almost certainly a translation slip) → use 200 m. The creature held a sword only in its manga debut. The caterpillar body's colour isn't described → verify.
- **Hyōten Hyakkasō count:** "100 petals" (character page) vs. "100 flowers" (battle page) → treat as 100 flowers.
- **Heights:** Hitsugaya 134 cm (main infobox) vs. 133 cm (anime-continuity infobox). Adult Hitsugaya has **no official height** (estimate given). Nozarashi Shikai "twice his size" per the wiki vs. ~1.5× in many panels.
- **Undocumented colours:** haori linings for Hitsugaya, Kenpachi and Mayuri (only Ukitake's crimson is stated); Hitsugaya's TYBW bandanna; zombie Hitsugaya's eye colour (red only in Brave Souls); Kenpachi's Bankai horn count/colour; Ukitake's reiatsu colour; Ukitake's eye colour differs (manga green / anime brown).
- **Unnamed / unshown:** Kenpachi's Bankai has no name and no release command (still unnamed in anime ep. 44); Ukitake's Bankai was never revealed anywhere; Ukitake's Hell design is unshown beyond the enlarged Shikai; Nozarashi's true form is never drawn (Kubo: an adult woman).
- **Databook / novel-only items** are marked inline (Ashisogi Jizō liquefying cuts — SOULs; Kenpachi's 10× eyepatch — Spirits Are Forever With You; Kenpachi keeping pace with Hikone's mixed Hohō and Mimihagi splitting Ukitake's Shikai — Can't Fear Your Own World).
- **Anime still airing:** TYBW Part 4 has episode pages through ep. 48; ep. 49 ("THE BLADE") is still empty — re-check for anime-only additions to Hitsugaya/Kenpachi/Mayuri (e.g. the final battle in Karakura) before locking kits.
- **Voxel scale:** the brief says 32 vox/m, PLAN.md §5.1 now says 48 vox/m for characters — both counts are given; weapon and summon sizes are in metres so they can be rasterised at either.
