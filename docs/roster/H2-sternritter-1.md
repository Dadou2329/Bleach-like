# Roster H2 — Sternritter (1/2)

Design dossiers for the first half of Yhwach's Sternritter: **Bazz-B** (H), **Äs Nödt** (F), **Bambietta Basterbine** (E), **Cang Du** (I), **Quilge Opie** (J), **Gremmy Thoumeaux** (V, with his creation **Guenael Lee**), **Mask De Masculine** (S, with his partner **James**), **NaNaNa Najahkoop** (U), **Driscoll Berci** (O), **BG9** (K) and **PePe Waccabrada** (L). The second half (the other four Bambies, Robert, the Lloyd twins, Nianzol, Berenice, Jerome, Shaz, Ebern) plus the **complete A–Z Sternritter index** are in `H3-sternritter-2.md`; Yhwach, Haschwalth and the Schutzstaffel are in H1.

**Conventions** (kept identical to H3 so the two halves read as one document)
- Heights → voxels at **32 vox/m** (rounded). Giants, meteors and large summons: build coarser (8–16 vox/m) and scale up.
- Hex colors are **estimates** unless marked **✔** (the wiki states that color in words). Anything marked **(verify)** must be checked against reference art before modeling. The colored manga paints most Quincy energy blue; the TYBW anime gives each Vollständig its own color — we follow the anime.
- Engine tags in `[brackets]` use the brief's vocabulary. Needs the brief's list does not cover are tagged `[NEW: …]` and collected in **§Engine features** at the end (identifiers reused from H3 where it is the same concept).
- **anime-only** (TYBW anime), **novel-only** (Narita's *Can't Fear Your Own World*, "CFYOW", and the short story *Beginning of the Revive of Tomorrow*), **Brave Souls** (mobile game) and **game-only** (our proposal, not canon) are flagged explicitly.
- **Canon power (1–10)**, roster-wide scale: 10 = Yhwach / Aizen / final Ichigo · 9 = Yamamoto, unleashed Kenpachi · 8 = elite captains, Schutzstaffel · 7 = captain-class Sternritter who beat a captain · 6 = captain-class with a quick or one-sided loss · 5 = lieutenant-plus / specialist.
- Sources: bleach.fandom.com character pages (linked per dossier), technique pages (Burner Finger 1–4, Burning Full Fingers, Burning Stomp, Shé Jìn Zhǎo, Star Flash, Star Flash: Super Nova, Star Lariat, Star Satsujin Punch, Love Kiss, Love Rope), battle pages and TYBW episode pages (their "Anime Notes" lists) named per dossier, and the shared Quincy pages (Quincy, Quincy: Vollständig, Sklaverei, Blut, Heilig Pfeil, Hirenkyaku, Shadow, Medallion, Schrift, Sternritter, Wandenreich). Fetched 2026-10-06 and paraphrased throughout.

**Answers to the assignment's question marks**
- **Bazz-B, "Burner Flame?"** — no technique of that name exists. His named moves are **Burner Finger 1, 2, 3, 4**, **Burning Full Fingers** and **Burning Stomp**; his flame waves, fireballs, flame walls, melting palm and counter-flame are unnamed. "Burner Flame" can be used as a **game-only** label for the generic flame wave.
- **Äs Nödt** — Vollständig **Tatarforas** (神の怯え) plus a giant body-horror transformation.
- **Bambietta** — Vollständig **Zofiel** (神の炎). The name never appears in the manga or the anime; it comes from official art at the *Bleach EX* exhibition, which labels her zombified version **"Zombiel"**.
- **Quilge** — the wiki romanizes his Vollständig as **Piskiel** (神の正義, ピスキエル); "Biskiel" is the same thing misspelled. He is a **Sklaverei** specialist: per Kubo, Piskiel has the highest reishi-capture capacity of any Quincy.
- **Gremmy** — he has **no Vollständig**; the whole kit is The Visionary (see the Imagination Menu in his dossier).
- **Driscoll did not have Ryūjin Jakka.** He medalized **Chōjirō Sasakibe's Bankai Kōkō Gonryō Rikyū** (lightning and weather). Yamamoto's Bankai **Zanka no Tachi** was taken by **Yhwach** (H1). Ryūjin Jakka, Yamamoto's Shikai, is what burned Driscoll to ash.
- **BG9's letter is K, but the Schrift was never revealed.** "The Knowledge" is fan speculation with no source; the kit below uses only its demonstrated cyborg and data-gathering abilities.
- **S: The Superstar** truly belongs to **James**. Mask is James's manifested "ideal hero" (Kubo Q&A #185), so James ships as Mask's companion.
- Vollständig of **Bazz-B, Cang Du, Mask, NaNaNa and BG9 are unnamed**. Cang Du's and BG9's were **never shown beyond the activation pillar**, so their looks below are **game-only** designs.

## 0.1 Shared Quincy / Sternritter layer (recap + H2-specific rules)

Everything in **H3 §1** applies unchanged: racial basics (reishi absorption, Hirenkyaku, air-standing, spirit weapons and Heilig Pfeil, Blut Vene/Arterie, Shadow, spiritual awareness, Hollow poison), the uniform, the Medallion, the Leiden Hant, the blue-flame arrival pillars, **Vollständig on T** (activation pillar breaks any prison; halo is a separate hitbox; stamina-limited) and **Sklaverei on hold T** inside Vollständig. Short recap of the control template:

| Input | Quincy default (dossiers override where noted) |
|---|---|
| Shift | Hirenkyaku: a thin blue-white reishi ribbon under the soles. Double-tap + lock-on = appear behind |
| RMB | Guard / parry; with Blut Vene on, guard becomes a "Vene Guard" (blue vein flash, chip damage only) |
| G | Blut Vene ⇄ Blut Arterie (never both; short gap when switching) |
| C (hold) | Reishi flare, Quincy style: ambient motes and loose voxel dust **spiral into** the user, then the aura flashes in their color; weak NPCs kneel |
| T / hold T | Vollständig / (inside Vollständig) Sklaverei; tap T again to revert |
| Tab | Quincy wheel: Shadow (teleport · gate · retreat), Medallion (steal Bankai), arrow variants |

Rules this file adds (they matter for five H2 characters who held a stolen Bankai):
- **Stolen-Bankai page.** While a medalized Bankai is held, Vollständig is locked (canon), so **T opens the "Stolen Bankai" page instead** (the Q–X row is replaced by that Bankai's moves at about 70% power). Canon details to keep:
  - A stolen Bankai **refuses to hit its true owner**: Cang Du's ice attacks curved away from Hitsugaya.
  - Thieves train with what they take, but it stays **weaker than the original**: Yamamoto felt Sasakibe's lightning was diminished.
  - A **Shin'eiyaku** (Hollowfied Bankai) poisons the thief and flies home. Cang Du's ice wing snapped off in a spray of blood, and BG9's systems failed.
  - `[NEW: CrossKitTheft]`
- **Auto-revive.** A defeated Sternritter's Vollständig can trigger by itself in a pillar and revive them at partial HP: BG9 reported "45% repaired", and Cang Du's pillar shattered the ice prison around him. Once per fight. `[NEW: AutoReviveTrigger]`
- **Story bookends.**
  - **Arrival:** every Sternritter steps out of a **pillar of blue fire**.
  - **Defeat:** losers get dragged to **Haschwalth's execution** (Cang Du, BG9).
  - **Endgame:** **Auswählen** beams strip every survivor's Vollständig (Bazz-B, NaNaNa).
