# Roster B2 — Gotei 13 captains (2/3)

Covers **Sōsuke Aizen**, **Kaname Tōsen**, **Sajin Komamura** and **Shunsui Kyōraku** (plus Nanao Ise as Shunsui's assist).
Source: bleach.fandom.com (character, technique, battle and episode pages, pulled through the API). Everything is paraphrased.

**Tag legend.** *(canon)* means manga. *(anime-only)*, *(game-only)* and *(novel)* mark material from those sources only. *(extrapolated)* marks a design proposal that canon never shows; use it to fill a kit slot, never as lore. Radii, durations and multipliers are **design proposals** unless a line says canon. Voxel counts use 32 voxels/m for characters. In the kit proposals, any input a form does not list carries over from the previous form.

**Captain haori (all four):** a white haori over the black shihakushō. The back carries the division number in black kanji inside the Gotei 13 rhombus. The hem has a band of black diamond/circle motifs. ## Sōsuke Aizen (藍染 惣右介) — ex-Captain of the 5th Division → traitor and master of Las Noches → Muken prisoner (TYBW ally of convenience)

- **Wiki:** https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Sousuke_Aizen · technique pages: https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Kanzen_Saimin · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/H%C5%8Dgyoku · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Kurohitsugi · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Gory%C5%ABtenmetsu · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Dank%C5%AB · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Raik%C5%8Dh%C5%8D · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Fragor · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Ultrafragor · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Teleportation · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Caja_Negaci%C3%B3n · https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Ky%C5%ABj%C5%ABrokkei_Kakaf%C5%ABmetsu
- **Roster tier:** **S** (core, ship first). He is the series' main antagonist: final boss of two arcs, the ally for the TYBW finale, and the owner of 8+ distinct looks. His illusion kit needs the *Perception Layer* tech, and Tōsen's dome, Shunsui's games and every later illusionist reuse it.
- **Canon power (1–10):** **10** in Hōgyoku forms and in TYBW; **9** as a sealed captain. Feats: alone, he cut down four captains and four Visored (two using Bankai), one or two strokes each. He blocked a Bankai blade with one finger. His unincanted Kurohitsugi (one third of its power) disabled a captain. As a lieutenant he stopped a Hadō #88 with an unincanted Dankū. He survived being split by Mugetsu. In TYBW, while confined to a reiatsu-limiting chair, he tore the Seireitei's sky barrier (Shakonmaku) with a Kurohitsugi. He hypnotised Yhwach while sealed in Muken.
- **Body:** 186 cm → **≈60 voxels** tall; 74 kg. Tall and lean, long legs, narrow waist, shoulders level and relaxed. He stands upright and moves without hurry: in fights he walks rather than runs (Soul Resurrección kept this). His sword is often held low and one-handed, left hand free. Silhouette keys by era:
  - Captain: square glasses, soft curtain bangs, long-sleeved haori.
  - Traitor: slicked-back hair with one loose strand, high standing collar.
  - Chrysalis: white cocoon with four tails.
  - 3rd Fusion: waist-length hair.
  - 4th Fusion: six wings and a flared dress.
  - Final: wing-sheets on tendrils tipped with skulls.
  - TYBW: wrapped in black leather bands, one eye covered, seated in a chair.

- **Look by form/outfit** (each must be readable at a glance):
  1. **Lieutenant (≈110 years ago, *Turn Back the Pendulum*):** the same curtain hair and square glasses, face a touch softer. Standard shihakushō. Lieutenant armband (5th) on the left arm.
  2. **Captain, 5th Division (Soul Society arc):**
     - Hair: soft shaggy curtain bangs parted at the centre, covering the forehead, ear-length at the sides.
     - Face: square black glasses; gentle eyes that droop slightly at the outer corners; a mild smile.
     - Outfit: long-sleeved white haori with "五" on the back and turquoise lining at the cuffs and front; sword at the left hip.
  3. **Betrayal reveal (Sōkyoku Hill):** the same captain outfit. He crushes or discards the glasses and sweeps his hair back with one hand. New hairdo: **slicked back with a single strand falling over the forehead**. The eyes become noticeably sharper.
  4. **Hueco Mundo I:** black shihakushō, red sash, and an open white Arrancar overcoat with narrow sleeves and a **tall standing collar** (white outside, black inside).
  5. **Hueco Mundo II / Fake Karakura (main villain look):**
     - White coat over a matching white tunic with black trim. At the front, below the waist, the tunic ends in a **square cut-out**.
     - Hakama-like trousers; pink (anime) / black (manga) sash carrying the sword on a metal plate.
     - Black Arrancar boots with white lining, read as a colour-inverted tabi/waraji.
  6. **1st Fusion:**
     - Same outfit. He opens his coat to show the **Hōgyoku embedded below the chest**.
     - Wounds heal behind **porcelain-like cracks** spreading from the orb.
     - Late in the stage, a white cloak-like substance flows from the orb and covers the body and **half the face**.
  7. **2nd Fusion — Chrysalis (蛹籃の時 Yōran no Toki):**
     - **Pure white shell from head to toe**; only the eyes stay uncovered (solid violet, no iris or pupil).
     - Tight above the waist. Below it the shell opens into **four long tails** with dark interiors.
     - Legs white down to feet shaped like heeled boots.
     - **Four violet lines run crosswise** from the Hōgyoku; one climbs to the face.
     - Later the **sword fuses into the right hand**.
     - Damage cracks and folds the shell like ceramic and shows a glowing violet substance underneath. The shell then heals smooth.
     - The head casing can be ripped open (Isshin's Getsuga) and keep fighting.
  8. **3rd Fusion:** the head casing breaks off and his own face returns. Hair now grows **to the waist**, loose, and keeps the signature strand. **Silver-white irises on purple sclerae**. Body: white suit with tails (unverified — the page only says he "still resembles his original form"; check the anime).
  9. **4th Fusion — "Winged/Butterfly":**
     - He steps out of a **violet pillar of reiatsu**.
     - Body: the white robe becomes a **full-length dress that flares to tattered edges** at the feet, fitted above the waist, with violet streaks at the neckline.
     - Face: **no irises, no eyebrows**, and a **black vesica-piscis mark on the forehead**. Hair even longer; the strand is gone.
     - Chest: a **gaping Hollow-like hole with a white cross; the Hōgyoku sits at its centre**.
     - **Three pairs of wings**; the rear pair has long tails. The front pair sharpen to points once he fights Ichigo.
     - The sword stays fused to the right hand.
  10. **Final Fusion — "Monster":**
      - The forehead mark splits into a **third eye** (black sclera, white iris) that bleeds and twitches. Then the face cracks and peels apart to the sides.
      - Under it: a **black, demonic, skull-like face** with two rows of bared teeth, no nose and **three white eyes**. The black runs down the neck.
      - Body: white, no clothing, defined muscle. **Three holes down the torso**, the Hōgyoku floating in the top one.
      - Limbs: **black lower legs ending in single spikes**; **black hands, left one clawed**; right hand fused to a **black sword**.
      - Wings: the six widen into **sheets, each with one huge eye** (white iris, black sclera) and long tails. Each hangs on a **thick tendril ending in a white, mouth-only skull**.
  11. **Reverted (post-Mugetsu):** back to the Hueco Mundo II look, slicked hair. Urahara's seal fires **cross-shaped beams out of his body** that merge into a **star-like barrier** around him.
  12. **TYBW — Muken prisoner:**
      - **Black uniform of wide, horizontal leather bands** bound together. The jacket part flares like a tunic below the knee, has white trim and fastens at the front. Black trousers and boots of the same material.
      - A **grey neckcloth sits on the shoulders like a capelet**; while he hangs crucified in Muken, **red stakes** pierce it.
      - Originally the bands cover the face. On release the band over the **left eye and the mouth** comes off; the **right eye stays covered like an eyepatch**.
      - **Seated and strapped into a special chair** that confines his reiatsu.
      - Hair long and loose past the shoulders (unverified — check the TYBW anime).
  13. *(game-only, optional skin)* Brave Souls "Hōgyoku × Kyōka Suigetsu":
      - Forehead third eye; floor-length hair; violet line markings from the eyes.
      - Black robe with white chest panel; the Hōgyoku ringed by four violet crystals in a void in the chest.
      - Detached floating arms; six mirror-faced wings.

- **Forms & states (ordered):**
  0. **Lieutenant** — same kit, lower numbers (prologue / flashback chapter).
  1. **Captain (sealed = Shikai)** — command **"Kudakero" (砕けろ, Shatter)**, spoken to release *and* dismiss. He can also release silently. Nothing about the sword changes. The release *is* the hypnotic ritual (see spec).
  2. **Traitor** — the glasses-off, hair-back reveal. Kit unchanged; story tools added (Garganta, Negación pickup, Arrancar summons).
  3. **1st Fusion** — Hōgyoku implanted → instinctive regeneration, ↑durability, ↑strength.
  4. **2nd Fusion "Chrysalis"** — begins when he hits his "limit as a Shinigami" (vs. Isshin). It completes after Urahara seals the reiatsu vents in his wrists and his own power explodes. Effects: white shell, blinding speed, shockwave, reiatsu no lower being can sense, sword fused.
  5. **3rd Fusion** — the chrysalis ends. His presence turns the weak and nearby matter to dust; debris floats around him.
  6. **4th Fusion "Winged"** — Gin's Kamishini no Yari dissolves a hole in his chest, Gin takes the Hōgyoku, and Aizen recalls it. A violet pillar rises from his chest and he evolves (he names conquering fear of oblivion as the key). Adds wings, shard-teleport and an incanted mega-Kurohitsugi.
  7. **Final Fusion "Monster"** — the Hōgyoku forces it when he rages at losing to a Human. Adds Fragor and Ultrafragor.
  8. **Reverted / sealed** — after Mugetsu he regenerates, but the Hōgyoku strips the evolutions away. Urahara's hidden seal (Kyūjūrokkei Kakafūmetsu) closes on him.
  9. **TYBW chair-bound** — leather seals released through **19 keyholes in the floor**; Shunsui was allowed 3 keys, and the master key is sealed in Shunsui's chest. The chair confines his reiatsu.
  10. **TYBW unbound** — Yhwach's black-reiatsu blast destroys the chair. Full Kidō (Hadō #99) and Kyōka Suigetsu return.
  - **Bankai: not revealed.** Do not invent one. (Novel trivia: *Spirits Are Forever With You* says it would only affect people already under Kanzen Saimin.)

- **Kanzen Saimin → game-mechanics spec (proposal; the core of Aizen):**
  1. **Exposure.** Each character has a persistent `ksExposed` flag kept in the save/profile ("once seen, forever").

## B2 summary — tiers, shared engine needs, open questions

**Tier recommendations**

| Character | Tier | Canon power | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sōsuke Aizen | **S** | 10 (9 sealed) | Main antagonist, 8+ looks, two boss fights plus a TYBW ally. His illusions drive the Perception Layer tech the others reuse. |
| Shunsui Kyōraku | **A** (S-candidate) | 8 | Captain-Commander; his rule-games are the most distinctive kit in the roster once the Rule-Field framework exists. |
| Sajin Komamura | **B** (→A) | 7 | The 100 m mirrored giant is the best destruction showcase. Promote once the giant tech exists. |
| Kaname Tōsen | **B** | 7 | A strong boss with a cheap Bankai once Perception Layers exist. The blind-camera gimmick is unique. |

**Engine features these kits need beyond the brief's primitive list**

1. **Perception Layers (per-observer reality).**
   - The server keeps the truth. Each client **and each AI** gets a filtered or augmented view: hidden entities, observer-only puppets, appearance swaps, render-only voxel overlays, and audio/HUD/lock-on masks.
   - The AI must target through its own layer.
   - Used by: Aizen (all illusions; Chrysalis imperceptibility), Tōsen (dome), Shunsui (Kageokuri realism, Bankai colour grade), Komamura (concealment).
   - Hidden data must never reach the deceived client (anti-cheat).
2. **Persistent per-character status across sessions** — the `ksExposed` flag, "once you've seen Kyōka Suigetsu, forever".
3. **Damage-event ring buffer with retroactive re-attribution** — Aizen's "since when" ultimate re-targets the last ~10 s of hits; Shunsui's Act 1 copies past wounds onto the partner.
4. **Rule-Field scripting framework.** A Field defines participants, a predicate evaluated each tick or event, and win/lose outcome hooks. Predicates needed:
   - Relative height — Takaoni.
   - Shadow-footprint overlap — Kageoni.
   - Impact-voxel colour vs. called colour, plus colour coverage — Irooni.
   - View cone × target velocity — Daruma.
   - Declared-pact violation — Yubikiri.
   - Hilt-contact state — Tōsen's dome.
   - Act sequencing with a two-performer requirement — Shunsui's Bankai.
   - A Vengeance meter that triggers a forced form change — Komamura.
5. **Shadow system on voxels:** per-entity shadow footprints from the dominant light; shadows as portals (enter or exit any shadow); a shadow pocket dimension that stores entities. Light sources must be able to erase shadows (the counter). The same pocket tech serves **Caja Negación**.
6. **Runtime voxel colour-coverage counts** per model, palette-family tagged. Dropped garments and destroyed voxels update coverage (Irooni; shedding the haori matters).
7. **Mirrored-pose giant summons:**
   - Skeletal retargeting of the player's pose to a ×35 summon with a root offset, plus transient limb-only spawns (Tenken).
   - **Bidirectional damage/heal link by body part**.
   - Coarse-voxel giant LODs (0.5 m/voxel) and far-field destruction.
   - Armour plates that detach as physics debris (Dangai Jōe).
8. **Fluid volumes with rule-modified buoyancy** (Act 3: swimming up sinks you) and reiatsu-drain drowning.
9. **Special camera/render modes:**
   - Sensory-deprivation black with a **contact-only "touch channel"** (Tōsen's dome).
   - **Reikaku View** (Tōsen's blind sight) switching to full colour in his Resurrección.
   - Mirror-shatter reveal post-effect (Kanzen Saimin).
   - Sky and skybox swaps: matsubame theatre panels, Kurohitsugi darkening, a crack in the Shakonmaku sky dome.
10. **Forced / auto transformations** on HP thresholds or story events: the Hōgyoku Fusions, including a revive into the 4th Fusion; Komamura's reversion to a wolf.
11. **Defeat rules beyond HP:**
    - **Immortal** (seal-only defeat): Aizen in Fusions and TYBW; Komamura while human.
    - `survivesDecapitation` flag, and **head-only body-part destruction** for Shime no Dan.
12. **Tier-based sensing, targeting and status nullification:** Aizen's 2D/3D imperceptibility and his reiatsu cancelling weaker abilities; Komamura's concealment against reiatsu sensors.
13. **Voxel decay / levitation auras:** the 3rd Fusion turns nearby matter to dust and orbits debris; TYBW contact disintegration.
14. **Damage tags plus reflection rules:**
    - A `divine` tag reflectable by Shinken Hakkyōken.
    - Push-back of in-flight projectiles (Dangai Jōe).
    - Beam cancellation by a wind ring (Bushōgoma).
    - Hilt-grab counters.
15. **Indiscriminate-ultimate safety:** ally-in-range checks, warnings and AI patience (Shunsui's Bankai); friendly fire caused by swapped identities (Aizen).
16. **Uninterruptible narration and chant system:** Kidō incantations that power up while you walk, the Bankai act poems, Shikai poems as callouts.
17. **Control-inversion auto-adaptation** — Aizen re-maps inputs after one hit.
18. **Space-time Kidō volumes** (incanted Kurohitsugi: time-slow plus gravity) and **reishi-fuelled spells** that consume nearby voxels (Goryūtenmetsu).
