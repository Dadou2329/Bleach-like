# Roster A1 — Ichigo Kurosaki & family

Design dossiers for the flagship **Ichigo Kurosaki** (every form, outfit and technique from chapter 1 to the TYBW finale, plus the anime-only Blood Chains form), his inner world (Hollow Ichigo / "old man" Zangetsu, with a recommendation to ship **Hollow Ichigo as his own playable**), his father **Isshin Kurosaki** (ex-captain of the 10th Division) and the bonus character **Kon** (lion plushie / Ichigo's body with super legs / Muscle Kon / Karakura-Raizer).

Conventions used below
- Heights → voxels at **32 vox/m** (rounded). Ichigo is **174 cm → 56 vox** until the Lost Agent time-skip and **181 cm → 58 vox** afterwards.
- Hex colors are **estimates sampled from reference images on the wiki** (median of pixel clusters from anime screenshots, normalized for scene lighting; TYBW-era items cross-checked with the official digital-color manga pages). Anything tagged **(verify)** should be checked against model sheets before modeling.
- Canon flags: **[manga]** canon, **[anime-only]** (original 2004–12 anime fillers *and* TYBW-anime additions 2022–26), **[movie-only]**, **[game-only name]** (a canon move that only games named, or a game invention). Unflagged = manga canon.
- Engine tags use the brief's primitive vocabulary in `[brackets]`. Needs **not** covered by the brief's list are tagged `[NEW: …]` and collected in **§6**.
- Sources (bleach.fandom.com via MediaWiki API, paraphrased): Ichigo_Kurosaki (+ /Anime_Continuity, /Other_Media), Zangetsu_(Zanpakutō_spirit), Zangetsu_(Quincy_Powers), Getsuga_Tenshō, Getsuga_Jūjishō, Saigo_no_Getsuga_Tenshō, Cero_Creciente, Blood_Chains, Reiatsu_Clones, Chain, Deadly_Darts, Hollowfication, Visored, Fullbringer, Blut, Cero, Sonído, Hohō, White, Isshin_Kurosaki (+ /Anime_Continuity), Kon (+ /Anime_Continuity, /Image_Gallery), Modified_Soul, and the battle pages *Ichigo vs. Ulquiorra (both fights)*, *Visored Training*, *Ichigo vs. Yhwach (all three)*, *Ichigo & Orihime vs. Yhwach*, *Isshin Shiba & Masaki vs. White*, *Isshin, Kisuke & Yoruichi vs. Aizen*.

## 1. Ichigo Kurosaki (黒崎 一護) — Substitute Shinigami; Human–Shinigami–Hollow–Quincy–Fullbringer hybrid (flagship)

- **Wiki:** https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Ichigo_Kurosaki · also /Anime_Continuity, /Other_Media · technique pages: Getsuga_Tenshō, Getsuga_Jūjishō, Saigo_no_Getsuga_Tenshō, Cero_Creciente, Blood_Chains, Reiatsu_Clones, Chain, Deadly_Darts · spirits: Zangetsu_(Zanpakutō_spirit), Zangetsu_(Quincy_Powers)
- **Roster tier:** **S** — the game's cover character and the only one whose kit spans five power systems (human martial arts, Shinigami, Hollow, Fullbring, Quincy). Ship first with K2–K3 (Shikai/Bankai/mask) and K7 (TYBW); the other loadouts are content drops that reuse the same rig.
- **Canon power: 10/10** at the end of the series (per-form ratings in §0). Feats: his golden true-Bankai Getsuga cut a Soul-King-empowered Yhwach in two (Yhwach survived only by rewriting the future); Mugetsu wounded a Hōgyoku-fused Aizen badly enough to trigger his seal; post-Dangai he caught Aizen's blade bare-handed and crushed a fully-incanted Hadō #90 Kurohitsugi; his Full Hollow form tore apart Ulquiorra's Segunda Etapa; a week of Royal Guard training took him from needing Bankai against one Vollständig to calmly handling four Sternritter (Candice in Vollständig) with Shikai only.
- **Body:** **174 cm → 56 vox** (to Fake Karakura), **181 cm → 58 vox** (Lost Agent onward; the post-Dangai look is "noticeably taller" too — use 58). Lean, long-limbed, athletic (gets visibly more muscular after becoming a Shinigami). Proportions at 56 vox: head ≈7 vox (chin→crown), shoulders ≈13 vox wide (14 at 58), crotch height ≈26 vox, arm shoulder→wrist ≈18 vox + 5–6 vox hands. Silhouette keys: **a spiky orange hair mass** that adds **+3–5 vox above the crown** and **2–3 vox of spikes at the sides and nape**; permanent **scowl** (brows angled down to the nose bridge — a 1-vox dark line is enough at this scale); **a sword as big as he is** on his back in most forms. Ambidextrous (mirrored animations are canon).
