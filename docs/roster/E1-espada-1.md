# Roster E1 — Espada 1–5

Design dossiers for the top of Aizen's Espada: **Coyote Starrk** (with **Lilynette Gingerbuck**), **Baraggan Louisenbairn** (with his six Fracción as summons), **Tier Harribel** (with the Tres Bestias + Ayon as summons), **Ulquiorra Cifer** (deep dive, incl. the Las Noches dome battle) and **Nnoitra Gilga** (with Tesra).

Conventions used below
- Heights → voxels at **32 vox/m** (rounded). Hex colors are *estimates* from the anime palette unless marked otherwise; anything marked **(verify)** should be checked against reference art before modeling.
- Engine tags use the brief's primitive vocabulary in `[brackets]`. Needs that are **not** covered by the brief's list are tagged `[NEW: …]` and collected at the end (Appendix B).
- Character blocks use the brief's dossier format (`## <Name> (<kanji>) — <rank/role>`); Fracción/summons get compact build sheets under their master; meta sections are "Quick reference", "Shared … kit" and Appendices A–C.
- "Anime-only", "game-only" and "non-canon" are flagged explicitly. Game-only names (e.g. *Camarada*) are used where canon never named the move.
- Sources are the bleach.fandom.com pages listed per character (fetched through the MediaWiki API). Text is paraphrased.

## Shared Arrancar / Espada kit (racial baseline for everyone below)

Every Espada in this file gets this baseline; per-character dossiers only note deviations.

- `Cero (虚閃, "hollow flash")` — charged beam of reiatsu; most need a pose and an emission point (mouth/hand/finger), color is personal. Power scales with the user. [Beam, Blast]
- `Bala (虚弾, "hollow bullet")` — hardened reiatsu fired like a bullet (usually from the fist): weaker than Cero but ~20× faster, can be chained, more concussive. Default crimson. [Projectile orb, multishot]
- `Gran Rey Cero (王虚の閃光, "flash of the king hollow")` — Espada-only: Cero mixed with the user's own **blood**; much stronger and faster, colored uniquely per user, **spins like a razor** and visibly **warps space** as it travels. Forbidden inside Las Noches. *None of the five Espada in this file is shown using it in canon* — put it in the Tab wheel as an Espada-wide option, costs HP. [Beam, Blast, NEW: ScreenSpaceDistortion]
- `Cero Oscuras (黒虚閃, "black hollow flash")` — Espada-only black Cero, **released state only**. Only Ulquiorra (and, in the manga only, Yammy) shows it. [Beam]
- `Sonído (響転, "echo turn")` — Arrancar flash-step, booming/static "bun" sound. Per Kubo (Klub Outside #685) it converts the body's reishi into "something that is not reishi", and per the novel it emits no reiatsu → **cannot be tracked by Pesquisa/sensing** (Ulquiorra uses exactly this to identify Sonído vs Shunpo). Map to **Shift**. [Teleport, Mobility, Sensing/Stealth]
- `Hierro (鋼皮, "steel skin")` — reiatsu-hardened skin that can stop a Zanpakutō; strength ∝ reiryoku (exception: Nnoitra). Can be "adapted to" — a strong enough or sharpened attack cuts it. Implement as a **per-hit damage threshold** rather than flat armor. [Stance/passive, NEW: DamageThreshold]
- `Pesquisa (探査神経, "probing nerve")` — sonar-like reiatsu sensing; locates and grades targets. [Sensing]
- `Descorrer (解空, "unfastening the sky")` — Espada-level gesture that tears open a stable **Garganta** (black void slit) to walk between Hueco Mundo, the Human World and Soul Society; it closes neatly behind them. Inside the Garganta, travelers make footholds out of reishi. [Teleport, NEW: PortalRender]
- `High-Speed Regeneration (超速再生)` — most Arrancar *traded it away* for power. Kept by **Ulquiorra** (sealed and released) and by **Nnoitra in Resurrección**. Brain and internal organs never regenerate. [Heal/Reject (self), NEW: LimbSever/Regrow]
- **Resurrección rules** (apply to every T release): the release **heals all prior wounds**; losing consciousness or being too injured auto-reseals (released form dissipates, sword reforms); a broken Zanpakutō = no release; discarding part of the released body means you can never reseal. Most releases make the Arrancar look closer to their Hollow self. An Espada's release can trigger **primal fear** in weaker Arrancar (Harribel's remark).
- **Las Noches laws:** Espada ranked **4 or higher may not release inside Las Noches**, and no Espada may fire a **Gran Rey Cero** inside — either could bring the fortress down. Game rule suggestion: top-4 Espada pressing T inside the Las Noches interior first trigger a "break out" cinematic (blast upward through the ceiling/dome) — this is exactly what Ulquiorra does.
- `Caja Negación (反膜の匪)` — Espada equipment: a small cube that seals a subordinate in a closed pocket dimension "forever"; an Espada caught in it breaks out within hours (Ulquiorra did). Optional Tab item for any Espada: trap a minion / NPC; on bosses it's a short time-out. [Bind, NEW: PocketDimension]
- **Anatomy notes for modelers:** every Arrancar has a **true hole** through the body (model it as empty voxels *and* exclude it from the hitbox — Nnoitra's gimmick depends on it) [NEW: HoleHitbox]; mask remnants float very slightly above the skin (Kubo Q&A #646) — a 1-voxel offset is canon-accurate; facial **estigma** marks are part of the "mask"; standard uniform = white jacket/hakama, black sash, black boots with white lines. On death, Arrancar **decompose into reishi** (dust/ash blowing away) [NEW: AshDeath].
- **Fracción** = an Espada's personal Números retinue; mobilizing them without Aizen's permission is an offense (story flavor for summon cooldowns).

### Set piece — the Las Noches dome battle (Ichigo vs Ulquiorra, final)

**Environment brief**
- **Las Noches** — a white fortress the size of a city (as large as the Seireitei or larger; Nel says it takes about three days to walk to a gate): a **central dome** ringed by towers, with **five smaller towers standing on top of the dome**. Inside, the dome's ceiling **simulates a blue daytime sky**; outside is Hueco Mundo's **permanent night** with a **crescent moon**, endless **white dunes** and quartz "trees". The walls are **not Sekkiseki** — breakable. Interior halls can be **rearranged** (a level-design hook for shifting corridors). Under Baraggan it had no roof or walls at all.
- **Orihime's tower** — tall white tower; her cell has one large rug and a high window; pillared floors below; leaning towers outside.

**Phases** (boss phases ↔ cutscenes; the player is Ichigo, with optional swaps to Uryū / VL Ichigo / Ulquiorra in versus mode)
1. **Sword duel in the tower** — Ichigo bursts up through the floor; Ulquiorra draws first. Thrust duel among pillars (misses gouge pillars). Ichigo reads him — his sword style is just his hand style with reach — and nicks him beside the "4"; Ulquiorra **splits the floor** in annoyance; Orihime's Santen Kesshun blocks a strike. Interlude: Loly & Menoly attack Orihime; Yammy barges in; Uryū's **anti-Arrancar landmine** drops Yammy through the floors. Ichigo masks up — Tensa Zangetsu bites into Murciélago; Ulquiorra exits through the wall.
2. **Vertical chase** — mid-air green Cero (Ichigo blocks it with his blade); Ulquiorra **sprints up the tower's outer wall** and **blasts through the dome from inside** — the fake sky breaks into real night.
3. **Murciélago on the dome (R1)** — release, green rain. Luz de la Luna rush (only an instinctive Getsuga saves Ichigo; part of his mask breaks); Luz throw cuts his shoulder; mid-air clash; Ulquiorra **asks for the strongest Getsuga** and tanks it; **Cero Oscuras** — mask gone, a big chunk of the dome gone; **wing slap** into a tower; Luz slash **halves the tower**; carried through another tower to its top.
4. **Segunda Etapa (R2)** — "the face of true despair". Uryū and Orihime ride a reishi platform up to the hole. Head-grab throw through a tower; tail whip; masked clash; slammed through two more towers; held up by the tail on a tower top; **point-blank Cero Oscuras through Ichigo's chest** (story loss state).
5. **Uryū's stand** (optional playable) — Hirenkyaku ambush, Heilig Pfeil barrage + **Licht Regen** (no effect); Uryū loses his left hand (off-panel); Seele Schneider charge; tail slash; Santen Kesshun shattered by the tail.
6. **Vasto Lorde** (optional playable) — Orihime screams; Ichigo rises as a **long-haired, horned full Hollow**. His red Cero (charged between the horns) **overpowers Cero Oscuras**; tears off Ulquiorra's left arm (regrown); **Lanza thrown into the desert → titanic explosion on the horizon**; VL's Sonído slips past Pesquisa; thrown severed arm vaporized by a Lanza (anime: a boulder); VL **grabs the Lanza and crushes it** (explodes in his hand); a slash across the chest takes Ulquiorra's left ear; VL stamps on his head and fires a Cero **straight down through the dome** — a pillar of light to the ground below.
7. **Aftermath** — VL goes to mutilate the body; Uryū grabs his wrist and gets impaled; Ulquiorra (missing his left arm and leg, organs gone) cuts off one of VL's horns with Luz de la Luna, the Cero misfires and Ichigo reverts; Ulquiorra accepts a "fair" rematch but his remaining wing crumbles to ash; asks to be killed; reaches toward Orihime and turns to ash ("so this is the heart").

**Engine hooks:** the dome is a **mega-structure** (LOD'd destructible shell; Cero Oscuras ≈ removing a huge spherical-cap chunk; Cero downward = a vertical shaft through the whole dome); **interior-sky ↔ exterior-night** switch at every breach; towers as destructible pillars with "thrown-through" character collisions; a **far-away desert impact** for the Lanza (long-range VFX + crater well outside the arena); ash-dissolve death; character swaps mid-fight.
