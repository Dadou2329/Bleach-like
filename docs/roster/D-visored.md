# Roster D — The Visored

Design dossiers for the eight Visored: **Shinji Hirako**, **Kensei Muguruma**, **Mashiro Kuna**, **Lisa Yadōmaru**, **Rōjūrō "Rose" Ōtoribashi**, **Love Aikawa**, **Hachigen Ushōda** and **Hiyori Sarugaki**, plus the **shared Hollowfication layer** (mask on/off, time limit, Cero) that they all run on and that Ichigo reuses.

Conventions used below
- Heights → voxels at **32 vox/m** (rounded). Hex colors are *estimates* from the anime palette unless marked otherwise; anything marked **(verify)** must be checked against reference art before modeling.
- Engine tags use the brief's primitive vocabulary in `[brackets]`. Needs **not** covered by the brief's list are tagged `[NEW: …]` and collected in §10.
- Source flags: **[anime-only]** = added by an anime adaptation (mostly the TYBW anime, 2022–2026); **[novel]** = *Can't Fear Your Own World*; **[game-only]** = video game, not canon; **(ext.)** = our gameplay extrapolation of a canon power, not shown on page/screen.
- Sources: bleach.fandom.com character pages, technique pages (Sakasama no Sekai, Bakudantsuki, Air Blades, Sandbag Beat, Mashiro Kick/Super Kick/Drop Kick/Super Cero, Arpeggio, Izayoi Bara, Sea Drift, Prometheus, Senmaioroshi, Panty-Flash Tornado, Nijū Ichijō Tonbokudari, Hifuki no Kozuchi, Kagamibiraki, Suikawari, Gatling Mad-Stomping, Super Harisen Slipper, all of Hachigen's Kidō), battle/event pages (Battle in Fugai Forest, Visored Training, the Fake Karakura Town fights, Kensei & Rose vs. Mask, The Gotei 13 & The Visored vs. Gerard Valkyrie) and TYBW episode pages (*The Fundamental Virulence*, *Rages at Ringside*, *Blood for My Bone*). ## 1. Shared Visored kit layer — Hollowfication (all eight; reuse for Ichigo)

### 1.1 Canon rules the system must honor
- **Who/what:** the Visored are Shinigami who conquered an inner Hollow (Aizen's experiment, stabilized by Urahara's Hōgyoku and later a vaccine). Unmasked they read as ordinary Shinigami; donning the mask merges a second (Hollow) power source on top of their Shinigami powers.
- **Donning:** a mask condenses out of white reishi over the face. Most bring a hand across/in front of the face; **Hiyori** summons it by will alone, **Hachigen** slides it on with one or both hands, **Mashiro** just shouts *"Henshin!"* with no gesture, Ichigo rips it down. They can also wear it **partially** — Hiyori pushes hers up onto the side of her head between bouts, Shinji half-lowered his to address Aizen.
- **While masked:** large boosts to strength, speed, durability and pain tolerance (Shinji: "like driving someone else's body"); Hachigen's boost goes into **Kidō power** instead. The **voice doubles** with a ringing Hollow echo. **Eyes:** sclera turns black; irises recolor (Shinji **grey**, Hiyori stays **brown**, default Hollow **yellow**). Reiatsu becomes "dual" — sensors read a masked Visored as Hollow/Espada-like.
- **Time limit:** about **3 minutes** for seven of the eight after a century of practice; **Mashiro** held hers 15 hours from day one, but in a hard fight (vs. Wonderweiss) it shattered after minutes, with no warning to her. Limits grow with training (Ichigo: 4 s → ~11 s → minutes). A broken/expired mask cannot be re-summoned right away (exact rule unknown).
- **Usage pattern (canon trivia):** Visored dismiss the mask right after a big attack to save time for the next one — Love pulls his on for Hifuki no Kozuchi and takes it off immediately.
- **Masks break under trauma:** Hiyori's stomps shattered Ichigo's; Wonderweiss's punch finished Mashiro's; Baraggan's Respira broke Hachigen's.
- **Cero:** every Visored has one with a personal color/emitter (table §1.3). In the TYBW anime all eight fire at once and the beams fuse on Gerard's head **[anime-only]**.
- **Mask + Bankai at the same time:** allowed (Kubo Q&A; Kensei does it in the TYBW anime).
- **Bankai theft:** being a Visored does **not** grant automatic immunity to Quincy Medallions — Shinji swallows a Shin'eiyaku before his Bankai in the second invasion **[anime]**. Don't give Visored innate theft immunity; the Shin'eiyaku item does it.
- **Resurrección:** theoretically possible for Visored (Kubo Q&A) but never shown → **not** in any kit.
- **Pre-mastery Hollowfication (Hollowfication Incident, story only):** victims cough up white liquid, a mask grows over the face, then the body mutates into a mindless, savage form (Kensei: hulking giant; Mashiro: insect legs); **agitation speeds the process up**; it spreads through wounds. Inner-Hollow battle lasts up to ~1 hour (Hiyori's record: **69 min 2 s**).

### 1.2 Mask state machine (G) — implementation

- **States:** Unmasked → Donning (char-specific, 0.25–0.6 s; Mashiro/Hiyori 0 s) → Masked → Doffing (instant, free) / **Shattered** (forced) → Lockout → Unmasked. Optional sub-state **Resting** (mask pushed up on the head: no drain, re-don in 0.1 s) — Hiyori's default, available to all.
- **Hollow Gauge:** 180 s. Drains 1 s/s while masked; each Cero costs extra (≈6 s, charged ≈12 s); heavy hits to the head chip it (2–10 s). Regenerates 1 s per 2 s unmasked → "pulse the mask" play is optimal, exactly like canon. At 0: voxel shatter, 1.2 s stagger, **12 s lockout**.
- **Mashiro exception:** gauge shown as "∞ (15 h)"; a hidden **Overexertion** meter fills from damage dealt/taken vs. enemies of Espada tier or higher; at 100% the mask shatters with almost no tell (cracks appear only at 90%).
- **Masked buffs (suggested, unbalanced on purpose):** +40% damage, +25% move/Shunpo speed, −30% damage taken, immune to light-hit flinch; per-character emphasis in each dossier (Hachi: barrier HP ×2 & cast speed ×1.5 instead).
- **Slot convention — "X is the Hollow slot":** unmasked, X holds a normal ability; masked, **X becomes that character's Cero** (tap = quick shot, hold = charged/sustained). Cero variants also live in the Tab wheel.
- **Presentation:** mask = separate voxel part on the `head` socket built at **2× density (64 vox/m)**; hair voxels inside the mask volume are clipped; eye material swap (black sclera + iris color); aura gets a black-crimson outer rim `#2A0B10` around the character's own color; voice DSP = original + pitch-down double + short metallic reverb. Crack decals at 50/25/10% gauge; shatter = chips that dissolve into white reishi motes.
- **Damage tag `MaskBreak`:** some moves deal bonus gauge damage (Hiyori's Gatling Mad-Stomping, decay fields like Respira, very heavy head hits).
- **Sensing:** while masked the character is flagged "Hollow" for Pesquisa-type sensing and Hollow-detecting gear.

### 1.4 Team & story systems
- **Visored Cero Convergence** `[NEW: BeamMerge]` **[anime-only]**: when ≥2 Visored Cero beams converge on one target within ~1.5 s, they braid into a single multicolor beam (+50% per extra beam) ending in a large dome Blast. - **Visored rotation (story/co-op):** in training they fought Ichigo's Hollow form in 10-minute shifts inside Hachigen's barrier — a "tag-team endurance" mission template.
- **Feral Hollowfication (story/boss only)** `[NEW: StatusContagion]`: AI-controlled transformation with no time limit; hits on Shinigami apply a spreading "Hollowfication" status (cough → white vomit → mask growth over ~60 s; faster while the victim attacks or takes damage = canon "agitation"). - **Shin'eiyaku:** consumable that protects a Bankai from Medallion theft for a battle (temporarily Hollowfies the Bankai); Visored use it like everyone else.

- Bone white `#EFEBE0`, shade `#C9C3B4`, deep recesses `#3A3631`; openings show the masked eyes (black sclera + iris glow). No colored markings are confirmed on any Visored mask → keep them white unless reference shows otherwise **(verify)**.

- **Sakasama no Sekai — gameplay spec ("fun, not frustrating")**
  Design goal: deliver canon's *"my own reflexes betray me"* without making the screen unreadable. The core trick: **invert where combatants are, not the whole screen.**
  1. **Exposure.** Spinning Sakanade emits mist volumes. Inside, hostiles gain **Scent** +35%/s (dense core) or +15%/s (fringe); each ring-spin hit adds +10%. At 100% → **Inverted** for 10 s (players) / 18 s (AI), refreshed while inside. No smell/breathing check. Shinji's allies are excluded by default; he can include/exclude any entity.
  2. **Mirror phantoms (core rule).** For a victim V with inversion matrix M = diag(sx, sy, sz) in V's local frame (−1 on each inverted axis), every *other* combat entity at P — characters, projectiles, telegraph decals — is shown to V at **P′ = V + M·(P − V)**, with its orientation mirrored too (y inverted ⇒ drawn upside-down: canon's "Shinji standing upside-down in mid-air"). The real bodies are hidden from V. **Terrain is not mirrored**, so moving around stays readable.
  3. **Real body, false reflexes.** V's inputs drive V's real body, but every *assist* reads the phantoms: lock-on, target snap, auto-facing, parry auto-orientation, directional guard, dodge-away-from-threat, AI aim. Players who lean on assists flail; players who reason "he's mirrored through me" can strike back — that is exactly the Aizen moment, and it feels earned.
  4. **Inverted feedback.** Damage-direction arrows, the hit flash on V's own body (injury location), threat indicators and 3D audio panning (**[novel]** hearing inversion) all use mirrored vectors; sensing skills (Pesquisa, echolocation, reiatsu sense) return phantom positions.
  5. **Axis presets** (canon: Shinji can flip only some directions): ALL (default) · ↔ only · ↕ only · front/back only · ↔+↕. Changing preset on an adapted victim is Shinji's mind game.
  6. **Adaptation (canon counter).** V gains +25% Adaptation per hit landed on the real Shinji or per correct block; elite AI also gains +2%/s (Aizen-class: +25%/s). At 100% the phantom turns translucent and a faint "true echo" of the real target appears → immune to that preset. A preset change drops Adaptation to 40%; it decays 5%/s outside the mist.
  7. **Hard counters (canon weaknesses).** Omnidirectional attacks (spherical blasts, 360° spins, fields, Bambietta-style carpet bombing) ignore inversion; complete-hypnosis-class illusionists are immune; deafening oneself does nothing.
  8. **Vertigo.** ±2° camera sway for players; AI stumbles/retches every ~4 s (1 s opening).
  9. **Readability & comfort.** Pink vignette + an "inverted compass" with three axis icons (hidden for partial presets until Adaptation ≥ 50%). Option **Reduced Vertigo** removes sway and the V camera flip but keeps phantoms. Players get at most 10 s of continuous inversion, then 3 s immunity; AI has no cap.
